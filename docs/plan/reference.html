From TeamTrack

What an earlier in-house tracker ("TeamTrack": a Svelte admin over a Deno API) taught us, at both the big-picture and the detailed level. A lot of this design is TeamTrack kept on purpose, and the rest is TeamTrack's known gaps, fixed.

The detail: behaviours worth copying

  • The URL is the state. ?ticket=, the view, and every filter. Anything you can see, you can link to.
  • Server-side authorisation on every call: load the board, resolve the role, assert a minimum. Role maths lives in a pure, unit-tested module (_tt-role.ts → can()).
  • A mention always notifies, whatever the person's mode, and never twice (it is removed from the comment recipients).
  • Never notify the actor.
  • Removing a stage in use asks where its tickets go. Here that becomes the server's remap, returning 409 without one.
  • Tags created on the fly; the same name counts as the same tag.
  • A limited mover's bulk action skips what they cannot move and reports it, instead of failing the whole batch.
  • Secrets are never sent to non-admins, and a general settings save cannot overwrite them. Here the secret is not stored in readable form at all.
  • A "Saving…" toast only after 250 ms, counted across in-flight writes.
  • Real names cached in IndexedDB. This becomes unnecessary here, because members are denormalised and live.
  • Floating, minimisable chat windows (Gmail-style) for keeping several threads open. This is worth keeping in phase 3.
